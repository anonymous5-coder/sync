# Universal Truth — Research Workspace

## Purpose

This directory is the repository-level umbrella for distinct, explicitly scoped domains and directions of research. It provides a common place to organize investigations that may be independent, overlapping, or interconnected.

The directory name describes the ambition to investigate fundamental questions; it does **not** imply that any included project has established universal truth, nor that every domain or direction must be investigated.

## Structure

- `Intelligence_research/` — the current, specifically scoped research programme concerning context, discovery, intelligence, recursive cognition and self-mastery. Its governing plan defines its own scope, investigations, verification requirements and acceptance criteria.
- Future domain directories — created only when a domain or direction is deliberately initiated and has a defined scope, questions, evidence standards and governing documentation.

## Organization principles

1. Keep each domain's research questions, source material, models, experiments and conclusions within its own directory.
2. Preserve shared concepts and cross-domain links explicitly; do not force all domains into one theory or one methodology.
3. Distinguish established knowledge, definitions, formal deductions, empirical findings, hypotheses, modelling assumptions and speculation.
4. Record dependencies and points of contact between domains without assuming that a connection is evidence of a causal or explanatory relationship.
5. Do not create empty placeholder domains merely to anticipate possible future work. Add a domain when its initiation and foundation are ready.
6. Each domain should have a clearly bounded scope and its own completion/readiness criteria.

## Scope of the current session

The active work remains confined to `Intelligence_research/`. Other domains are intentionally left unopened; this restructuring only establishes the shared parent workspace.
